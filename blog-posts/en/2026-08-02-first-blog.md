# First blog

This is an old dream of mine — building a real, good math-teaching app. It won't happen all at once. Right now I'm mostly in the preparation stage, laying groundwork before the real building starts.

One thing that's already fixed, and won't change: the idea behind it. Helping people learn math, in a way that's actually enjoyable — through clarity, simplicity and understanding.

I plan to post updates here every week: what's happening, what's working, what isn't.
